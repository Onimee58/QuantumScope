## A note on the VQE example

The H₂ Hamiltonian in Algorithms → VQE is the standard two-qubit Hamiltonian in the STO-3G basis at a bond length of 0.735 Å. Its exact ground energy, −1.137306 Hartree, matches the known value for this basis. The ansatz is the hardware-efficient form of reference 16.
